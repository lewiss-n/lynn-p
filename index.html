( About )

Great talent doesn’t sell itself. It needs someone who knows its worth, speaks the brand’s language and makes sure the work gets delivered. That’s the job. I do it well.

My background is in commercial sales: 18 months of target-driven client work at two of Kenya’s biggest banks, KCB and Co-operative Bank, plus a degree in Banking & Finance. I bring that discipline to talent: clear numbers, tight contracts, payments tracked to the last shilling.

Then there’s the creative side. I’ve booked artists for stages and brands across Kenya, built rate cards and proposals, and co-produced Echoes, a live music experience, from line-up to sponsors to show night. I know what happens behind a booking, and what it takes to make one go smoothly.

( How a deal runs )

From brief
to bank alert.

  1. 01

    Brief

    I learn what the brand or organiser needs: audience, goals, budget, timelines and must-haves.

  2. 02

    Match

    I put forward the right talent with a rate card and proposal built around the brief, not a generic price list.

  3. 03

    Negotiate

    Fees, deliverables, usage and requirements agreed in writing, so nobody is surprised later.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Talent briefed, schedules locked, venues and production teams coordinated. I stay on it until the work is live.

  5. 05

    Close & repeat

    Payments settled, expectations met on both sides, and a relationship ready for the next deal.
